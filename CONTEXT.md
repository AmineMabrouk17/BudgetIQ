# BudgetIQ

BudgetIQ is an AI-powered personal finance app: track income, expenses, and assets, with a Gemini-powered assistant that logs transactions from natural language.

## Language

**AI assistant**:
The built-in chat feature that parses natural language and offers to log income, expenses, or assets. Not marketed as a generic "chatbot".
_Avoid_: chatbot, bot

**Income**:
Money coming in (salary, freelance, gifts) that a user tracks.
_Avoid_: revenue

**Expense**:
Money going out (bills, food, subscriptions) that a user tracks.
_Avoid_: spending, cost

**Asset**:
Valuable things a user owns (cash, car, investments) that they track.
_Avoid_: valuables

**Budget planner**:
A positioning term the product does NOT claim — the app tracks money but has no budgeting/limits feature.
_Avoid_: using this on the landing page or in marketing copy

**Display currency**:
The currency the user selects in the app header. Every money figure in the dashboard is converted and formatted in it. Conversion is display-only — stored amounts are never converted.
_Avoid_: currency setting, active currency

**Category**:
A free-form label on a transaction (Food, Transport, Salary). Canonicalized on write: trimmed and Title-Cased, so "food " and "FOOD" both become "Food".
_Avoid_: tag, label

**Savings rate**:
The share of this month's income not spent: (income − expenses) ÷ income.
_Avoid_: (none)

**Base currency**:
The single currency stored amounts are implicitly denominated in. Fixed at USD for now — the display-currency dropdown converts from it. No per-transaction currency is stored.
_Avoid_: (none)
